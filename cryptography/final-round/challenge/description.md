# Close Primes

- **Category:** Cryptography
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Time Limit:** 20 minutes

---

### Description

Our security intern was tasked with deploying a standard RSA encryption service to safeguard confidential communications. Eager to impress the team with faster key generation, the intern decided to take a clever little "shortcut" during the prime selection process.

Along with the encrypted transmission, the intern left behind the encryption script with a self-assured comment:

> *"I made my own prime generation... I'm sure it's secure enough :)"*

We intercepted the encrypted ciphertext and the public parameters. Can you exploit the intern's shortcut to factor the modulus and recover the secret flag before time runs out?

### Hint

> *"Sometimes the shortest distance between two points reveals everything."*
